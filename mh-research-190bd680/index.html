Keeping it raw

Your question: how do we add data videos without losing subscribers who love that everything is raw, real and made without AI? Below is what the evidence says. Every number links to its source.

Short answerKeep your voice and your face, and add simple charts in the middle. YouTube judges each video on its own, your audience clearly wants human-made content, and studies show a raw talking head plus simple visuals keeps people watching.

1 · Will a new format hurt the channel?

YouTube judges each video on its own

YouTube official
“What can lead to a decline in overall channel views is when viewers stop watching most of your videos when they’re recommended to them.”

So one new video can't drag the channel down. YouTube shows it to the people who respond to it. The risk only appears if most videos stop working for your viewers. That's why every new video keeps you on camera and in your own voice.

YouTube Help, “If one of my videos under-performs, is that going to hurt my channel?” · support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805
YouTube official
“It’s important that you always keep experimenting with new topics and formats. To build an audience, creators need to both retain their existing viewers and attract new ones.”
Same page · support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805

3 · Does raw actually keep people watching?

Raw beats polished. Face plus visuals beats either alone

Peer-reviewed · 6.9 million video sessions
“Videos produced with a more personal feel could be more engaging than high-fidelity studio recordings.”
“Videos that intersperse an instructor’s talking head with slides are more engaging than slides alone.”

This is exactly the plan: your raw, personal style, with a simple chart when a number matters. The same study found engagement drops on longer videos. On the other hand, the competing videos that win on these topics run 9 to 18 minutes (see section 5). So each video gets the length its topic needs, between 8 and 12 minutes, and nothing is padded.

4 · The format

What stays, what's new

Stays exactly as now

  • You on camera at the start and the end, filmed on your phone
  • Your own voice for the whole video
  • Your team's phone clips from real life in Vietnam
  • No filters, no stock footage, no actors

New

  • Simple, plain charts and maps when a number matters
  • The source shown on screen for every number
  • A clear structure, so people stay to the end

Safeguards

  • The new videos get their own playlist, and your normal videos continue
  • Each video asks a different question, with different charts. YouTube penalises content that “looks like it’s made with a template”
  • After 2 videos we compare watch time, subscribers per 1,000 views and comments. If your audience doesn't like it, we stop
YouTube monetization policy, “inauthentic content”, 15 Jul 2025
